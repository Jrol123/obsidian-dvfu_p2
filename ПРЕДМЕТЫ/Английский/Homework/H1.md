## № 4 (p.15-16)
1. **EVIDENCE** — one or more reasons for believing that something is or is not true
2. **PERSPECTIVE** — a particular way of considering something
3. **EXPERIMENT** — one of several parts of someone's character, a situation etc.
4. **EXPERIMENT** — a test done in order to learn something or to discover if something works or is true
5. **HYPOTHESIS** — an idea or explanation based on known facts but not yet proved
6. **EQUATION** — a mathematical statement in which two amounts are shown to be equal
7. **DIVERSITY** — the fact that there are many different ideas or opinions about something (a diversity of opinion)    
8. **TECHNOLOGY** — «(the study and knowledge of) the practical, especially industrial, use of scientific discoveries» — это определение слова technology
9. **INVESTIGATION** — an official attempt to find out the truth about / the cause of a crime, accident, or scientific problem
10. **FACT** — something that is known to have happened or to exist, especially something for which proof exists
11. **EXPLANATION** — the details or reasons that someone gives to make something clear or easy to understand
